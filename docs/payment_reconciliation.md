# Payment Reconciliation

Document ID: DEMO-002
Version: 1.0
Scope: Fictional portfolio procedures only.

## Matching records
Compare the merchant reference, invoice reference, amount, currency and payment date across the payment and invoice records.

## Amount mismatches
Investigate partial payments, fees and adjustments when amounts differ. Do not automatically treat every mismatch as an error.

## Missing payments
Check the settlement record and processing status when a payment is missing from the invoice ledger.

## Unresolved differences
Record the discrepancy and the checks performed. Refer unresolved differences to the reconciliation team.

## Completion
Mark a reconciliation as complete only when the records agree or the difference has a documented, approved explanation.
