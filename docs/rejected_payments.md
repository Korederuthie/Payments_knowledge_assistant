# Rejected Payment Submissions

Document ID: DEMO-001
Version: 1.0
Scope: Fictional portfolio procedures only.

## Initial checks
For a rejected payment submission, check the rejection reason, submission date, merchant reference and payment amount.

## Invalid account details
If the rejection reason indicates invalid account details, ask the authorised operations team to verify the details through the approved secure process. Do not request full bank details through chat.

## Insufficient funds
If the rejection reason indicates insufficient funds, refer the case to the collections team for review. A rejection alone does not authorise a retry.

## Duplicate submissions
Check whether another submission exists for the same merchant, invoice and amount. Escalate suspected duplicates before any further submission.

## Escalation
Escalate unknown rejection reasons or inconsistent statuses to payment operations. Include the submission reference and rejection code.
